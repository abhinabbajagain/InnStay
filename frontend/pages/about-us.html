About InnStay

Our Mission

InnStay is dedicated to making travel more accessible and enjoyable for everyone. We connect travelers with unique accommodations and experiences around the world.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, InnStay started with a simple idea: to revolutionize the way people find and book accommodations while supporting local hosts and communities worldwide.

Our Values

Trust & Safety

We prioritize the safety and security of our community members.

We welcome travelers and hosts from all backgrounds and cultures.

Sustainability

We're committed to responsible travel and environmental protection.

Community

We support local economies and cultural exchange.
