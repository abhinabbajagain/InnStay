Cancellation Options

Our flexible cancellation options help protect both guests and hosts.

Cancellation Policies

Cancel up to 30 days before check-in for a full refund. After that, 50% of the booking amount will be refunded.

Cancel up to 14 days before check-in for a full refund. Between 14 and 7 days before, 50% will be refunded. Within 7 days, no refund.

Full refund if canceled 60 days before check-in. 50% refund if canceled 30-60 days before. No refund for cancellations within 30 days.
Important Notes
  • Each property may have different cancellation policies
  • Service fees are non-refundable
  • Check your booking confirmation for the specific policy
  • Contact support for special cancellation requests
