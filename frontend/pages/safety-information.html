Safety Information

Your safety is our top priority. Here are important safety guidelines for using InnStay.

Before Your Stay

  • Read the property listing carefully and review host ratings
  • Communicate with hosts through our platform before booking
  • Verify the property address and amenities
  • Share your itinerary with trusted contacts

During Your Stay

  • Keep doors and windows locked
  • Be aware of your surroundings and local areas
  • Don't share personal information with other guests
  • Report any safety concerns immediately

Emergency Support

If you experience any safety issues during your stay, contact us immediately at safety@innstay.com or call our emergency line.
