Host FAQ

Find answers to common questions about hosting on InnStay.

Your earnings depend on your property type, location, availability, and pricing. Most hosts earn between 30-70% of their nightly rate after InnStay's commission.

You must be at least 18 years old, have a valid government ID, provide accurate property information, and pass our verification process.

All hosts are covered by our Host Protection Insurance. You can file a claim through your dashboard if damages occur during a booking.

Use the calendar in your host dashboard to mark dates as available or blocked. You can also set seasonal pricing to maximize earnings.

Payments are processed 48 hours after guest check-in. You can withdraw to your bank account or InnStay wallet at any time.
