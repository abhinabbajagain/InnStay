Help Center

Welcome to InnStay Help Center. Here you'll find answers to common questions about using our platform.

To book a stay, search for your destination, select your dates, and choose from the available properties. Click "Search" to see all options, then select a property and follow the booking process.

You can cancel your booking from your account dashboard. Go to "My Bookings" and select the booking you want to cancel. The cancellation policy will be applied based on your booking terms.

You can contact our support team through email at support@innstay.com or use the contact form on this website. We typically respond within 24 hours.
